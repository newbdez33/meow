iOS · Two simple choices

Edit Widget has separate Sound and Character fields. The system owns both pickers. Each widget keeps its own pair.

Android · Make your own pair

Choose a character, audition a sound, and save both together. Keep it at 1 × 1, or resize to 2 × 2. Playback uses a media notification.

Platform constraints & implementation notes

iOS. Recommend an iOS 17+ widget extension while the app stays on iOS 15+. Use Button + AudioPlaybackIntent to play in the app process without foreground navigation. System Small is the smallest Home Screen widget. Tinted mode here is a visual approximation.

Android. Use a per-widget configuration and a short-lived media playback foreground service started by the widget tap. Stop the service and remove its notification when playback ends. Validate cold starts, audio focus, and launcher sizing on real devices.
