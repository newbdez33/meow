Reference for the tip sheet artwork. The app composes the hero at runtime from tipMug, one of five cats from the app (c02 black, c01 gray, c04 orange, c19 tabby, c21 orange with a bowl) and tipCan. The mug and the can, and the navigation bar icons navCup and navCat, were generated with the Codex CLI image generation tool; the untouched originals and prompts live in design/imagegen/.
