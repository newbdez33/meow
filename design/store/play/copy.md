## en-US
- App name: Meow simulator
- Short description (≤80): 27 cat sounds. 7 widget characters. A little meow on your Home Screen.
- Full description:
Meow is a pocket full of cats. Twenty-seven of them, each with its own mood and its own voice: purring, snoring, hissing, a hungry little kitten, a roar borrowed from a lion. Tap a cat and it meows back.

Use it to tease the cat on the sofa, or just because it is fun.

• 27 cats, 27 different meows
• Home Screen widgets: pair any of 7 characters with any of 27 sounds, then tap to play
• Set each widget separately, resize it, and preview sounds before you choose
• Works offline, nothing to sign up for
• Share the app with a cat person from the top right
• Free with one small banner ad. A one-time treat for the author's cat (or a coffee for the author) removes the ads for good on every device with your Google account

Available in English, Chinese and Japanese.
