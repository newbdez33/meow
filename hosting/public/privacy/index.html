Privacy policy

This policy covers the Meow app for iPhone and iPad (the app) and this website. The app is made and run by an individual developer, Jacky. Contact: newbdez33@gmail.com.

1. The app collects nothing about you

There is no account. Which cat you tap and which sound plays stays on your device and is never sent to the developer. The app contains no analytics SDK.

2. Ads (Google AdMob)

The app shows a Google AdMob banner at the top of its main screen. To serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud, the Google ads SDK may process your IP address and approximate location, device and advertising identifiers, your interactions with ads, and diagnostic information. Google and its advertising partners may use cookies, device storage and similar technologies.

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the app first shows a consent screen where you can accept, decline, or choose by purpose and partner. You can change that choice later through the hand icon in the top bar, which appears whenever it applies.

On iOS the app asks once for tracking permission. You can change it any time under Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. Declining does not limit the app; ads may simply be less personalised.

On Android the app uses the advertising ID through the Google Mobile Ads SDK. You can reset or delete it under Settings › Google › Ads (the path varies by device). The consent screen above is shown on Android too, and where regulations require it the ✋ button in the app reopens it.

See also how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google privacy policy.

3. Removing ads

If you choose the one-time ad-removal purchase inside the app, Apple's App Store handles the payment. The developer never receives your card or payment details. The app checks the purchase on your device to hide the banner and lets you restore it. Apple keeps the purchase record under its own policies.

On Android, Google Play handles the payment in the same way: the developer never receives your payment details, the app checks the purchase on your device, and Google keeps the purchase record under its own policies.

4. Sharing

The 🐱 button at the top right opens the system share sheet and hands the store link (App Store or Google Play) to the app or contact you choose. The app does not learn where you shared it.

5. Changes and contact

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date above. Questions go to newbdez33@gmail.com.
